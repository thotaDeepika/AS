# Faculty Application Form Fields

> This document describes the **23 categories** presented to faculty members in the appraisal application form, as defined in `FINAL_SCORING.md`.

## Structure Overview

The appraisal form is structured into **three primary sections**:

1. **TEACHING (Category 1)**: Covers instructional quality via the FCI Score.
2. **RESEARCH (Categories 2-12)**: Evaluates publications, patents, research guidance, and funded projects.
3. **SERVICE (Categories 13-23)**: Assesses professional development, institutional services, and outreach.

### Score Distribution by Designation

| Section   | Assistant Prof | Associate Prof | Professor |
|-----------|:-----------:|:-----------:|:------:|
| Teaching  | 60          | 50          | 40     |
| Research  | 10          | 20          | 30     |
| Service   | 30          | 30          | 30     |
| **Total** | **100**     | **100**     | **100** |

---

## TEACHING (1 Category)

### 1. FCI Score
- **Input**: Percentage (0-100)
- **Scoring**: ≥85% → 100%, ≥80% → 90%, ≥75% → 80%, ≥70% → 70%, <70% → 40% of Teaching max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF required

---

## RESEARCH (11 Categories)

### 2. Non-paid Refereed Journal Papers (SJR/Scopus/Web of Science)
- **Input**: Number of papers
- **Scoring**: 1 paper ≥ 1 → 100% of Research max
- **Attachments**: Up to 2 PDFs

### 3. Indexed Conference Papers (SJR/Scopus/Web of Science)
- **Input**: Number of papers
- **Scoring**: Per paper — AP: 50%, AssoP: 25%, Prof: 20% of Research max
- **Attachments**: Up to 2 PDFs

### 4. Non-paid Non-refereed Journals and Non-indexed Conferences
- **Input**: Number of papers
- **Scoring**: Any count > 0 → 10% of Research max
- **Attachments**: Up to 2 PDFs

### 5. Books/Chapters
- **Input**: Books count + Chapters count (composite)
- **Scoring**: 1 book = 50%, 1 chapter = 20% of Research max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 6. Disclosures Filed
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: 1 disclosure = 10% of Research max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 7. Patents Granted
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: 1 patent = 50% of Research max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 8. Research Guidance UG
- **Input**: Number of batches
- **Scoring**: 1 batch = 1% of Research max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 9. Research Guidance PG
- **Input**: Number of batches
- **Scoring**: 1 batch = 3% of Research max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 10. Research Guidance PhD
- **Input**: Number of batches
- **Scoring**: 1 batch = 7% of Research max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 11. Funded Projects
- **Input**: Amount in Lakhs (₹)
- **Scoring**: ≥10L → 100%, ≥5L → 50%, ≥1L → 30%, <1L → 20% of Research max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 12. Consulting Projects
- **Input**: Amount in Lakhs (₹)
- **Scoring**: ≥10L → 100%, ≥5L → 60%, ≥1L → 50%, <1L → 20% of Research max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

---

## SERVICE & PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT (11 Categories)

### 13. Conference Chair, Session Chair, Reviewer of Q1/Q2 Journal
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: Any count > 0 → 5% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 14. FDP/Seminar/Workshop organized as coordinator
- **Input**: Number of days
- **Scoring**: ≥5 days → 10%, ≥3 days → 5% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 15. Invited Technical Talks outside the Institute
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: Any count > 0 → 10% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 16. Events Participated Outside Institute
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: Any count > 0 → 10% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 17. Events Participated Inside Institute
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: Any count > 0 → 5% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 18. Industry Relations (MoU, Co-hosted event, Technical Talk Series)
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: Any count > 0 → 10% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 19. Institutional/Departmental Services (NBA/NIRF)
- **Input**: Role dropdown (Coordinator / Member)
- **Scoring**: Coordinator → 20%, Member → 5% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 20. Other Services to Institution or Society Contribution
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: Any count > 0 → 3% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 21. Awards and Honours
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: 1 event = 15% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 22. Professionalism / Team Spirit
- **Input**: Count
- **Scoring**: Any count > 0 → 2% of Service max
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF

### 23. Any Other Major Contributions
- **Input**: Free text (max 500 characters)
- **Scoring**: No automatic scoring (reviewed manually)
- **Attachments**: 1 PDF
